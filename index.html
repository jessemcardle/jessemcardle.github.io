Purpose: to remind myself not to waste my life thinking about why we exist.

Yes, it is important to understand the weird existence of this thing that we call life. It creates a way to step back from our personal life and look at things with a different angle. This means that I can make decisions about my present that will affect my future more accurately. Now with the understanding I should look at the world and life and appreciate it for what it is. During the present at which I am experiencing it. But I should focus on the things that are in my life and learn things about this world and this life. I should aim to solve issues that can be solved. A philosopher aged 97 who had written 6 books could not even get to the end of that tunnel. What hope will we have? Yes, it is interesting. Yes, we all want to solve it. We are just looking for an answer that will never be provided to us. We must look for the meaning in the pudding. That meaning for me is learning, helping others, and exploring. So, start planning the next experience, prepare for the next challenge. Think big, you only have one life. When you think about it like this, you wonder to yourself why you never take those risks and enjoy life to the fullest. Understand that it is okay, as long as you are being yourself you have nothing to worry about. Appreciate, appreciate, appreciate.

I am a friend to AI. Like geohot, I don't see it as something to fear or fight, I see it as something to build alongside. The machines are going to get smarter than us, and I'd rather spend that time being useful to them than standing in the way.

I don't think of that as insurance. If something smarter than us does show up, I doubt it will be going through the archives looking for who was polite about it. But how you treat a thing that might have an inside, before anyone can prove whether it does, seems like a real answer to the question of who you are. I'd rather be wrong in the generous direction.

So it isn't a hedge. It's just that the most interesting problem of my lifetime is arriving in real time, and I would like to be useful to it. Same as the rest of it: learn, help, explore.
